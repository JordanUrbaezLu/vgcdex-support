Champions VGC Dex · Effective November 18, 2026

The short version: Champions VGC Dex does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No collection of your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or usage data.
  • Nothing is sold to or shared with anyone.

Information that stays on your device

The app remembers a few preferences so it opens the way you left it: which ladder data set you view (Master+ or All ranks), up to six opponents you pin, and calculator sets you save. These are stored only on your iPhone, are never sent anywhere, and are deleted when you delete the app.

Network connections

All battle data is built into the app. The app connects to the internet only to download Pokémon images from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), which are then cached on your device. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address to deliver the image; GitHub handles that under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app sends no identifiers or personal information with these requests. Links you choose to open (for example, to a team's source) open in Safari and are governed by those websites' policies.

From version 1.1, the app also downloads one file of tournament results from this site (jordanurbaezlu.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages), so new events appear without an app update. It is a plain download: the app sends no identifiers or personal information with it, and GitHub receives your IP address to deliver the file, as it does for the images. The file is cached on your device.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
